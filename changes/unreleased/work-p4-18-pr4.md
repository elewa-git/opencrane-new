# Saved welcome survey requests

Members can save four checked survey steps, resume saved answers and restart with
an up-to-date progress position. Editing an earlier step removes later current
answers. Encrypted history stays saved. Original-command status checks make no
writes, and conflicting devices cannot confirm different answers under one id.

These back-end routes make no model calls, start no assistant work and spend
nothing. Survey screens and persona approval are still to come.
