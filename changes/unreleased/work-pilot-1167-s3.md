Operators can update a populated pilot after a new backup verifies, keep its
sign-in, chats and shared files, and return failed updates to the recorded images.
Updates repair the computer gateway and restart computers on source-checked images.
Rollback checks the original installation and refuses expired scanner approvals or
an overlapping backup. Computer source stays readable after private extraction.
Fleet tasks cannot update or roll back a pilot, including when their task marker is empty.
Operators can resume an interrupted rollback with its saved lock. A backup Job waiting for
that lock cannot block recovery. New computer homes survive update and rollback checks,
and cancelled image builds force their child processes to stop after a grace period.
