Operators can follow one guide to switch the demo to the tailnet pilot, invite
people, verify a backup and restore the demo if the switch fails. The packaged
installer accepts the dedicated pilot sign-in credential from a private file.
Invited people can open their link, sign in and accept in the browser.
Provider-mode recovery rebuilds the routines tables included in its backup.
Owners can still open the member list and invite people when only the event
position for the directory totals is unavailable.
