- A private worker can revoke a saved personal OAuth account at its configured provider and retire its earlier token copies, even after member removal. It recovers unsaved refresh replies and revokes the newest refresh token and every retained access token once. Lost replies remain unknown and never trigger another attempt. Public routes and automatic provider revocation remain later work.
- Successful OAuth refreshes keep old access tokens privately until their saved expiry so later revocation can invalidate them too. Missing unexpired material prevents completion. Routine maintenance removes expired access-only copies, while refresh-token cleanup still finishes after member removal.
