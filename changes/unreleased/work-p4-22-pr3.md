Owners and admins can record provider choices and pauses for assistants or the silo default, inspect real assistant counts and recover saved results. The first verified connection becomes the automatic default when its chat model is enabled and no choice or pause is saved. Administrators can explicitly choose any eligible connection. Concurrent retries return the saved result, and changed actors or requests cannot reuse its command id. Routine gaps stay explicit. These records do not yet change ordinary model calls or enable retirement.
