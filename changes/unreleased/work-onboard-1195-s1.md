New members open the welcome survey after signing in, including from a workspace
link, and can return to their saved progress. After they finish or skip setup,
sign-in opens the workspace page they asked for.
Packaged installs keep the survey, persona and Memory keys across restarts, so
members can save and view facts in the Memory panel.
A new member's computer stays awake during setup for up to one hour from joining.
Their first chat message ends the extra time, wherever they send it.
