Admins can review, publish and grant ordinary third-party HTTPS tool servers without
OpenCrane-specific cost or version declarations. Assistants can use the reviewed tools;
a changed tool list blocks the call and asks for admin review. Calls omit agent details,
and reported cost is recorded after the reply without inventing a missing amount.
Servers declaring OpenCrane details retain their strict checks. The unused Odoo
application is removed, and product examples describe generic business systems.

Rejected tools remain part of the reviewed list, and their OpenCrane declarations
keep the whole server under strict checks. Ask cards support empty and long tool
descriptions. Repeated result callbacks refuse conflicting cost reports.
Older saved catalogues keep their original digest rule during policy reads and rebuilds;
new discoveries record the full-list rule explicitly.
