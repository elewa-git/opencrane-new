Owner appointments now wait for confirmation on the successor’s member record.
Pending appointments grant no ownership. A competing removal is ordered against
confirmation, and step-down still requires another active, confirmed owner.
Background workers finish saved requests after restart. Existing installations
need a stopped-service handler upgrade and member and permission rebuilds.

Role changes and team turns now check only the member records that decide their
access. A former successor’s ordinary role change no longer interrupts another
person’s request. Membership history is read before the two-second write check.
