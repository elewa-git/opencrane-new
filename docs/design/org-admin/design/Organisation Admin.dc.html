Weekly stock report

Skill · version 3 · edited yesterday by Peter Otieno
What it does
Shown to people when they choose a skill.
Summarise stock levels per branch and flag items below reorder point.
Instructions
The assistant follows these when the skill runs.
1. List the warehouses for the chosen branch. 2. Get stock levels for every product in those warehouses. 3. Flag anything below its reorder point, with the shortfall. 4. Reply with a short table, worst shortfall first.
